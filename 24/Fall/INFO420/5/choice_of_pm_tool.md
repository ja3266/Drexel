# Choice of PM tool Discussion

# Task 
Write a discussion board post answering the following questions:

- What PM tool would you prefer to use given a lot of AI based tools out there? 
- In your view does the tool determine the success of the project or the likelihood of success? 
- How much of a sofware PM's day do you think will be affected or impacted by the PM tool?

THEN ANSWER TWO CLASSMATES. 

# Answers

1. What PM tool would you prefer to use given a lot of AI based tools out there? 

I would prefer to use Jira because of its flexibility, integrations and popularity. Jira seems to be the most popular PM tool among software development teams, and that is for good reason. It offers an array of features that are flexible with a multitude of different workflows, which is handy if you have teams that may have differnt styles. It also has great integrations with other software and fantstic APIs that allow for easy automation and integration with other tools in and out of the Atlassian suite of tools. I have used Jira for all three of my co-ops and in my current job so I would love to learn how it works from a PM's perspective. 

2.  In your view does the tool determine the success of the project or the likelihood of success? 

The tool does not determine the success of the project. Tools, at the end of the day, are tools and can only assist in reaching the goals of solving any problem. For example, if I have to hang a picture, I could easily use the most obvious tool, a hammer, to hammer in the nail to the wall to hang the picture on. I could just as easily use any other blunt object to hammer the nail into the wall. Some tools may work better than others but really should not determine the success of the project. 

3. How much of a sofware PM's day do you think will be affected or impacted by the PM tool?

A software PM's day could either be marginally affected or not affected at all by the choice of PM tool. I think that the choice of the PM tool can affect the initial setup of a team or project because of the potential limitions on workflow choices based on the tools used or cost of the tool, etc, however ideally the tool should streamline day-to-day operations. If a large amount of time is spent messing with the PM tool to create tasks, assign tasks, visualize schedules, etc, then a different tool should be chosen.
